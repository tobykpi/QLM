Planning Your Move

From first details to moving day.

  1. Share the details.Send your locations, preferred date, and an overview of your belongings. Photos help us understand bulky or unusual items.
  2. Review your estimate.We'll follow up with a free quote and next steps. Discuss access, special handling, and the help you need before confirming arrangements.
  3. Prepare for moving day.Confirm the agreed services, arrival arrangements, and building requirements with the team. Keep essentials and important documents with you.
Care & Preparation

Plan ahead for the items that matter.

A useful estimate starts with the details that are easy to overlook. Let us know about fragile items, tight spaces, and building rules before moving day.

  • Furniture and fragile items: Send photos and dimensions. Discuss wrapping, padding, packing materials, and any disassembly needed for your move.
  • Your home and building: Flag narrow doorways, stairs, elevator reservations, and floor or doorway protection requirements.
  • Larger moves: Include a room-by-room inventory and any heavy or oversized pieces so the team can assess the resources and scheduling needed.

Common Questions

Before you book.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Include your preferred moving date, pickup and delivery ZIP codes, what needs moving, and any stairs or access restrictions. You can attach up to four photos, with a combined size of 10MB.

Does submitting a request book my move?

The form requests a free estimate. The team will follow up with a quote and next steps; confirm scheduling and booking arrangements directly with QuickLift Moving.

Can I ask about packing and furniture protection?

Yes. Describe fragile pieces, furniture that may need disassembly, and any packing help you need. Confirm which materials and protection methods are included in your estimate before booking.

How do I check licensing and insurance details?

Contact the team before booking to request licensing and insurance documentation relevant to your move. If your building requires a certificate of insurance, share its requirements in advance.

What if I have a large move or oversized items?

Send an inventory, photos, approximate dimensions, and details of access at both locations. Ask the team to confirm capacity, equipment needs, and availability for your job.

How can I make a payment?

Use the QuickLift Moving payment link to view the available payment options. Confirm the amount and payment timing with the team first.
